    G-One: a multi-agent assistant that does real tasks

    Most chatbots stop at the answer. G-One goes further. A central router sends each request to a specialist agent (Email, Research, Finance or DeepSearch), and a deterministic validation layer checks every action before it runs. That gate cut wrong actions more than any prompt tweak I tried.

    • React / Next.js
    • FastAPI
    • MongoDB
    • Ollama + Mistral
    • LoRA adapters
    • Voice input

    Built during the Vodafone (VOIS) and Edunet Foundation internship, guided by Prof. Sudhanshu Gonge. The widget at the top of this page is a tiny, rule-based version of the same idea.

    Crop suitability from satellite data, with SEER Bharat Foundation

    Choosing what to plant is one of the biggest bets a farmer makes each season, and it's often based on habit or guesswork rather than data about their own land. Working with SEER Bharat Foundation, an NGO in India, we're building a tool that turns satellite data into a plain answer: which crop suits this piece of land best.

    • Pick a spot. Enter coordinates or simply mark the location on a map.
    • Read the land from space. The system pulls satellite imagery and environmental parameters such as rainfall for that area.
    • Get a recommendation. A model predicts the crops best suited to those conditions, so farmers can plan with data instead of guesswork.
    • Satellite imagery
    • Rainfall and climate data
    • ML prediction
    • Interactive map
    • Full-stack

    The problem

    When someone feels unwell, the first questions are simple: is this serious, and where do I go? For many people in India those answers are hard to get. Health apps are mostly in English, mostly text, and assume you know which facility to visit. Someone more comfortable speaking Telugu, Hindi or Marathi than typing English is left guessing.

    What we built

    A voice-first healthcare assistant you just talk to, in your own language.

    • Talks in many languages. Natural, spoken replies built on ElevenLabs voices, so it doesn't feel like reading a form.
    • Reachable where people already are. You can talk to it on a regular phone call or chat with it on WhatsApp, with no new app to install.
    • Points you somewhere real. It suggests the nearest health station, so the conversation ends with a place to go.
    • Connected to Trello through its API.
